## Data and media sources

Nothing here is redistributed: the console links to the source that published an
asset and quotes the licence that source stated.

| Source | Role | Terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wikimedia Commons | images, video, audio | per file — quoted on every result |
| Openverse | CC-licensed images and audio | per work — quoted on every result |
| Internet Archive | audio, video, archived pages | per item — quoted, absence stated |
| NASA Image and Video Library | imagery, footage | NASA media guidelines |
| GDELT, Hacker News (Algolia), Wikipedia | reporting and context | attributed, linked |
| Apple Music catalogue (iTunes Search), Deezer | released-music previews | official 30-second previews, store link only |
| Jamendo (optional key) | Creative Commons music | per track — quoted on every result |
| Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash (optional keys) | stock photography and footage | per their licences, quoted |
| Pwned Passwords (HIBP range API), RDAP/RIR registries, Certificate Transparency logs, Cloudflare/Google DNS-over-HTTPS | investigation | public endpoints, rate limits respected |

Wikipedia text is CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC0 and is attributed on every result. Puter.js
is loaded only when the free in-browser model is enabled in Settings; it is
served by Puter, and the briefing evidence sent to it is described in the README.
